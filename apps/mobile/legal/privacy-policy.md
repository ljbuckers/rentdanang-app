# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** March 9, 2026
**Effective Date:** March 9, 2026

## Introduction

RentDaNang Team ("we," "us," or "our") operates the RentDaNang mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our App.

## Information We Collect

### Information Stored Locally

- **Saved Listings:** When you save a rental listing, it is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data never leaves your device.
- **Language and Currency Preferences:** Your selected language and currency settings are stored locally on your device.

### Information We Do Not Collect

- We do not require account creation or login.
- We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or payment information.
- We do not track your location.
- We do not use cookies or tracking pixels within the app.

### Information Automatically Collected

- **API Requests:** When you browse listings, your device makes requests to our API (api.rentdanang.app). Standard server logs may record your IP address and request timestamps. These logs are retained for up to 30 days for security and performance monitoring.

## How We Use Information

- To display rental listings from publicly available sources in Da Nang, Vietnam.
- To maintain and improve the performance of our services.
- To monitor for security threats and abuse.

## Third-Party Services

The App displays rental listings aggregated from publicly available sources. We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties.

## Data Retention

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data to retain. Server logs with IP addresses are automatically deleted after 30 days.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the App and on our website.
